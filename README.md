## 📺 What Are Subtitles?

Subtitles display dialogue and other audio information as text. They can help viewers:
- Understand movies and TV shows in different languages.
- Follow dialogue in noisy environments.
- Watch foreign-language content.
- Improve language comprehension.
- Make video content more accessible.

## 🔎 How to Find Subtitles

1. Identify the movie or TV show you want to watch.
2. Search for its title using a subtitle discovery website.
3. Check the release version and video quality to find a compatible subtitle file.
4. Make sure the subtitle language matches your preference.
5. Load the subtitle file in a compatible media player.

## 🛠️ Subtitle Compatibility Tips

Subtitle synchronization and compatibility can depend on the video release.

- **Match the release:** Different cuts and releases may have different timings.
- **Check the language:** Choose subtitles in your preferred language.
- **Check the format:** Common subtitle formats include SRT, ASS, and VTT.
- **Adjust synchronization:** If dialogue and subtitles do not match, use your media player's subtitle delay controls.
- **Use trusted sources:** Download files responsibly and scan unfamiliar files when appropriate.
